Trail debris sprites — 21, 4 categories

Individual FLUX-painted items at 1024², rembg-matted to transparent. Composited into GroundPainting.svelte by the deterministic debris-field.ts field (terrain + trail-progress + Fort-Laramie spike + graves∝deathCount). Quality gate per spec: the three grave-* sprites must read somber, not comical. Total payload ≈ 612 KB.

natural (7) — everywhere; buffalo-chips weighted to treeless plains

bones (5) — mid-trail onward, ×1.5 on plains, ×0.6 in forest/mountains

junk (6) — ramps late + Camp-Sacrifice spike (Gaussian σ=0.05) at Fort Laramie

graves (3) — sparse, scaled by run's actual deathCount (capped at 0.6) — somber gate
