## New mechanics

- **#294** — [H] Rework hunting while in a wagon train — historical lookup needed. **Period reality (research):** trains genuinely DID organize hunts as a large-party activity, not as a solo errand. Joel Palmer 1845 explicitly describes the "hunting party" assembled at sundown for the next morning's hunt — captain picked 4-8 men with horses + rifles, sent them out at dawn while the wagons rolled, rendezvoused at the noon stop with whatever they shot. Bryant 1846's Russell Party did the same on the buffalo plains; Marcy 1859 codifies it as the standard practice. The kill was divided by household, not by hunter — period-explicit equity rule, the captain enforced it. Solo hunting did happen but mostly for small game (antelope, prairie chickens) — buffalo/elk were always group hunts because one man + one rifle couldn't bring back the meat alone. **Mechanic**: when the player presses Hunt while in a train, surface a "hunting party" choice instead of the current solo-shooter screen. Options: **lead the company hunt** (your hunter + N companion-wagon hunters; bigger haul scaled by combined firepower; meat divided by alive-soul count across the train; +train morale), **send your hunter alone** (current behavior; full haul to your wagon; small −train morale because you broke the equity rule), **stay with the wagons** (no hunt, just mileage). Pairs with #287 named profiles (Joe Meek and the Hunter-led wagons drive higher group-hunt yields), #122 fresh game meat distribution (the share-out math), #285 captaincy (the captain calls the hunt; non-captain players propose).

- **#279** — [H] Animal injury audit — replace single ox.health/fatigue model with discrete injuries. Period reality: emigrant teams suffered specific named injuries each with its own recovery profile, not the current "rest to top up health" model. Audit list (from Marcy + emigrant diaries): **lameness** (foot rot, gravel-cracked hooves) — needs shoeing + 3-7 days rest, can recur; **yoke galls** (harness sores on neck/shoulders from poor fit or overuse) — needs salve + days off the yoke, comes back fast under load; **bog injury** (sprained leg from soft ground/quagmire) — recovery 1-3 weeks, often kills; **snake bite** (rattlesnake on summer prairie) — 30% kill rate, needs hartshorn/laudanum + rest; **rinderpest / blackleg** (epizootic at posts where teams cluster) — slow death, contagious to nearby oxen; **broken leg** (river crossing, mountain descent) — period-realistic outcome was a bullet. Goes for mules too. Plus **shoe wear** as a continuous stat (current `shod` boolean is too binary — should degrade with miles, accelerated on rocky terrain). Hoosier-Trail-specific: ox/mule injury system intersects #277 (Teamster bonus), #283 wagon-train shared labor, and the dead `injuredOxen` field if present.
