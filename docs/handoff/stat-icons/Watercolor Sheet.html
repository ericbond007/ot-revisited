Provenance

Four glyphs preserve the silhouette of existing OT.IO art so the visual handoff from the emoji set is recognizable:

  • Pace (ox head) — simplified from explorations/travel-scene/ox-team.jsx's OxHead. Long flat profile, single forward-curving horn, big leaf ear, pied rust crown patch over cream body, cream muzzle. The 3/4 body coords (~ x=−8 to −14, y=−9 to −13) were re-traced into the 24×24 viewBox with the same proportions.
  • Rations (drumstick) — direct match to the 🍖 emoji used throughout party-panel.html. Round meat ball bottom-right, narrow bone shaft rising up to a double-lobed cream knob top-left — the unmistakable cartoon drumstick. Rust-brown meat wash with a warm highlight, ink overlay.
  • Cash (folded notes) — silhouette of the existing 💵 emoji. Folded stack with the top edge slightly lifted and a center-oval medallion, sage-green wash to read as paper currency not coin.

The other four — day, date, morale, health, water — are drawn fresh in the same wash + ink vocabulary as the landmark icon set. The morale concertina is period-correct (1840s emigrant trail instrument); the date's rust-tinted "today" dot is the load-bearing motif.
