# #1046 C2 — Wire the Company-Rest Decision Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) tracking.

**Goal:** Wire C1's pure `companyRestDecision` into the live engine so a captained train travels or lays by **as one coherent unit (player + every NPC wagon)**, replacing the per-persona in-train rest gate. No dissent UI yet (slice B) — the player silently follows the company call with a log line.

**Architecture:** One per-day chokepoint in `tickDayPausable` computes the decision, advances the `companyDecisionBlock` hysteresis state, and gates BOTH `applyTravel` (player miles) and the `traveled` flag passed to `advanceTrain`. `tickNpcWagon`'s #937 per-persona voluntary-rest block is bypassed when a `companyRestMode` is threaded through `NpcTickContext` (in-train); solo wagons keep the per-persona path unchanged.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Vitest, SvelteKit. VCS = **jj** (not git). Branch: `feat/1046-c2-wire` (current working change off the C1-merged master).

**Locked design decision (forced by the spec's premise):** A company lay-by is a **stationary day for the whole company including the player**. The player is bound to the company until dissent (slice B). On a lay-by day `applyTravel` is skipped for the player and `advanceTrain` gets `traveled=false`; the day still advances (date +1, consumption/conditions/ox-recovery tick) exactly like a player-initiated rest day. Solo (no `wagonTrain`) path: entirely unchanged.

**Scope:** C2 only. After this lands, the spec mandates a **governance-only sweep checkpoint** (characterize the arrival shift from coherent company rest, recovery math still untouched) before slice B is planned.
