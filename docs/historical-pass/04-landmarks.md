# Oregon Trail Landmarks — Independence MO to Oregon City OR

Cross-reference for landmark gaps. Mileages aggregate Wikipedia's *Route of the Oregon Trail*, OCTA mileposts, NPS Oregon NHT, WyoHistory, and Legends of America; figures vary by source (±10 mi at most landmarks, ±25 mi by Oregon City because route choice diverged). Where two routes existed (north/south Snake, Sublette vs Bridger, Columbia vs Barlow), the dominant 1843–1860 route is given.
