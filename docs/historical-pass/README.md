## Headline findings (for the impatient)

- **Epsom salts: real.** Standard period item, milder purgative + wound soak. We don't have it.
- **Calomel poisoning: already modeled.** Period-correct.
- **Buffalo chips:** universal plains fuel; we don't model gathering.
- **Stray oxen morning hunt:** the single biggest unmodeled time sink (2-4 hr daily delays, diary-attested).
- **Wagon-churned butter:** real and delightful — milk cow + crock = passive butter.
- **Native ferries + grass tolls:** primary form of Indian contact, not violence. We have tribe relations but not these encounters.
- **4th of July at Independence Rock:** ready-made set-piece event tied to the calendar.
- **Diary library doable:** ~12 unambiguously public-domain diarists with online transcriptions. Avoid Holmes's *Covered Wagon Women* (editorial copyright).
- **Landmark gaps:** ~25 missable POIs we don't have (Lone Elm, Vieux's Crossing, Windlass Hill, Mormon Ferry, Martin's Cove, Big Hill, Massacre Rocks, Salmon Falls, Burnt River Canyon, Laurel Hill, etc.).

## Pop-history myths confirmed

- **Pianos jettisoned at Independence Rock:** real, widely diary-attested.
- **"Saw the elephant":** culturally important but oversaturated — diary library skips it.
- **Conestoga wagons on the trail:** myth. Farm wagons. Conestogas were freight wagons too heavy for oxen.
