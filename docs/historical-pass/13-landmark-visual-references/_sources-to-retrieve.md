# Primary Sources to Retrieve

This file aggregates every "couldn't fully verify / not web-accessible" caveat surfaced across the 27 landmark research docs. Each item gives:

- **What** — the specific primary source
- **Where** — the institution / collection / catalog ID
- **Unlocks** — which landmark research(es) benefit
- **How to retrieve** — best-path (library card, interlibrary loan, paid scan, direct curator contact, etc.)

Organized by retrieval-difficulty and impact. Highest-impact first.

## ⭐ Highest impact (a single retrieval unlocks many landmarks)

### J. Goldsborough Bruff — 1849 sketches and journal manuscripts

- **What**: Bruff was an obsessive sketcher and diarist on the 1849 trail. His sketches survive across at least three institutional collections. Multiple research files flagged "Bruff specific quote not retrieved" for: Ash Hollow, Courthouse & Jail Rocks, Scotts Bluff, Independence Rock, Register Cliff, Devil's Gate, Soda Springs (Beer Springs HM 8044 items 82 + 84 confirmed cataloged but not image-accessible).
- **Where**:
  - Huntington Library, San Marino CA — primary Bruff sketch collection (HM 8044 series for Soda Springs etc.)
  - Yale Beinecke Library — additional sketches + diary fragments
  - Library of Congress — selected items
- **Unlocks**: 7 landmark research files (Ash Hollow, Courthouse & Jail, Scotts Bluff, Independence Rock, Register Cliff, Devil's Gate, Soda Springs)
- **How to retrieve**: Best path is the published edition Read, Georgia Willis (ed.) *Gold Rush: The Journals, Drawings, and Other Papers of J. Goldsborough Bruff*, Columbia University Press 1944 (out of print). Interlibrary loan via local public library. Alternative: institutional reading-room access at Huntington (San Marino) or Beinecke (New Haven) — both accept short-term reader applications.

### Merrill J. Mattes — *The Great Platte River Road* (Nebraska State Historical Society 1969)

- **What**: The definitive single-volume scholarship on the eastern half of the Oregon Trail. Multiple research files cited "Mattes" via secondary sources because the full text isn't web-accessible.
- **Where**: Nebraska State Historical Society Press; widely held in research libraries; out-of-print first edition + 1987 reprint
- **Unlocks**: North Platte 1 (Lower California Crossing), Fort Kearny, Ash Hollow, Courthouse & Jail Rocks, Chimney Rock, Scotts Bluff, Robidoux Trading Post — essentially every Platte landmark
- **How to retrieve**: Interlibrary loan. Most state university libraries have at least one copy. Also available used on AbeBooks for ~$20-40.

### Merrill J. Mattes — "Robidoux's Trading Post at Scott's Bluffs" (*Nebraska History* 1949)

- **What**: Foundational scholarly article on the Robidoux post, referenced by every subsequent source. The PDF returned binary-encoded to web fetch.
- **Where**: *Nebraska History* magazine, 1949 issue, via Nebraska State Historical Society archives
- **Unlocks**: Robidoux Trading Post (verify Möllhausen 1851 sketch details, exact post operational dates)
- **How to retrieve**: Direct request to NSHS at history.nebraska.gov — they reproduce scholarly articles on request. Free.

## 🎨 High-value period art images (web-cataloged but image-inaccessible)

### Paul Kane — Fort Walla Walla sketches (July 1847)

- **What**: Kane was a Canadian artist who traveled with the HBC across the West 1845-48. He visited Waiilatpu and Fort Walla Walla **four months before** the Whitman Massacre — his Fort Walla Walla sketches are the single best visual primary source for the adobe HBC fort.
- **Where**: **Royal Ontario Museum** (Toronto), Paul Kane Collection
- **Unlocks**: Fort Walla Walla (the most important visual primary source for the active HBC state)
- **How to retrieve**: ROM has a digital collection; specific Walla Walla sketch may require curator-request for high-res scan. Email collections@rom.on.ca. Often free for scholarly use.

### William Henry Jackson — Fort Bridger photograph (1860s+)

- **What**: WH Jackson photographed Fort Bridger during the Army-post era; the photo would be the best visual reference for the 1858+ rebuilt Fort Bridger.
- **Where**: **BYU Special Collections** (indexed digital catalog but image not web-accessible) + possibly LOC
- **Unlocks**: Fort Bridger (1858+ Army state)
- **How to retrieve**: BYU Special Collections (lee.lib.byu.edu) accepts scan requests. Cite the indexed catalog entry. Free or nominal fee.

### Frederick Möllhausen — Robidoux Trading Post sketch (1851)

- **What**: The ONLY known period visual of Robidoux Trading Post. Basis of the 1993 Gering reconstruction. Referenced by every secondary source but never publicly digitized.
- **Where**: Unknown private or institutional collection. Mattes 1949 article (above) likely identifies it.
- **Unlocks**: Robidoux Trading Post (only period visual)
- **How to retrieve**: Backstop is the Mattes article (item above). If Mattes locates it institutionally, follow up there.

### Nancy Ann Osborn Jacobs — Whitman Mission sketch (c. 1847)

- **What**: The only known on-site survivor sketch of the Whitman Mission compound. Inscribed as a "correct representation."
- **Where**: **Oregon Historical Society** (OHS), Mss 5326
- **Unlocks**: Whitman Mission (active-state architectural layout verification)
- **How to retrieve**: OHS digital collections (ohs.org) — likely accessible online with free account. Otherwise scan request.

### Alfred Jacob Miller — Devil's Gate watercolor + oil (c. 1837 + c. 1858-60)

- **What**: TWO documented Miller works for Devil's Gate (the agent confirmed both via the AJM Online Catalogue): Walters watercolor c. 1858-60 (vertical interior composition) + oil-on-board exterior approach c. 1837.
- **Where**: **Walters Art Museum** (Baltimore), Alfred Jacob Miller collection
- **Unlocks**: Devil's Gate (period composition reference); also general Miller archive serves Independence Rock, Sweetwater country
- **How to retrieve**: Walters has open digital collections at art.thewalters.org. Specific item images typically free. Search "Miller Devil's Gate" + "Miller Sweetwater."

### William Henry Jackson — multiple landmark photographs

- **What**: WHJ photographed many trail landmarks 1866-1900+. Specific items flagged:
  - California Crossing painting at Gilcrease Museum (depicts UPPER California Crossing — not Lower; useful but mislabeled)
  - Guernsey Ruts photos at LOC items 2017685124 + 2017685129 (existence confirmed, content not retrieved)
  - Ash Hollow painting (possible — not confirmed)
  - Chimney Rock pre-1872 photographs (must be pre-1872 for our period — 1872 lightning strike truncated the spire)
  - Fort Hall ruin photographs (late period)
- **Where**: Library of Congress (most), Gilcrease Museum (Tulsa OK), Scotts Bluff National Monument museum
- **Unlocks**: Multiple landmarks
- **How to retrieve**: LOC items are theoretically web-accessible at loc.gov/pictures — but require LOC reader account for higher-res. Worth one batch scan request.

### Osborne Cross — 1849 lithograph of Fort Boise

- **What**: The primary period visual of HBC Fort Boise.
- **Where**: **UW Digital Collections** (University of Washington Libraries), collection: alaskawcanada, id: **1475**
- **Unlocks**: Fort Boise (sole period visual primary)
- **How to retrieve**: digitalcollections.lib.washington.edu — likely free download. URL is confirmed.

### Samuel C. Mills — 1858 Fort Kearny photograph (Simpson Expedition)

- **What**: Earliest surviving photograph of Fort Kearny; shows the post as adobe with ZERO fortifications (validates "no stockade" prompt direction).
- **Where**: Likely in Simpson Expedition records — LOC or Nebraska State Historical Society
- **Unlocks**: Fort Kearny (visual primary; confirms architectural state)
- **How to retrieve**: Search LOC + NSHS for "Simpson Expedition 1858" or "Mills Fort Kearny."

### William Quesenbury — 1851 Devil's Gate sketch

- **What**: Quesenbury sketched along the trail 1850-51. The Devil's Gate sketch is referenced in WyoHistory but the repository is not identified.
- **Where**: Unknown — research needed (possibly University of Arkansas where Quesenbury later taught)
- **Unlocks**: Devil's Gate (additional period composition)
- **How to retrieve**: Start with University of Arkansas Special Collections.

### 1863 Moellman sketch + Collins plan — Fort Caspar

- **What**: Two best contemporary visual records of the Casper crossing area.
- **Where**: Archival collections — not identified by the agent (likely Wyoming State Archives or Casper-area museums)
- **Unlocks**: Fort Caspar / Guinard's Bridge era
- **How to retrieve**: Start with Wyoming State Archives + Fort Caspar Museum (Casper WY).

## 📜 Primary diary/journal texts (text format issues — likely solvable)

### Joel Palmer — *Journal of Travels Over the Rocky Mountains, 1845-1846* (1847)

- **What**: Palmer's full journal text — primary for many landmarks but LOC PDF returned binary
- **Where**: Library of Congress + Google Books + Internet Archive + Oregon Historical Society
- **Unlocks**: Bear River, South Pass, Sweetwater, Fort Hall, multiple others
- **How to retrieve**: Try archive.org for OCR'd plain text; if that fails, Google Books usually has searchable scan. Free.

### John C. Frémont — Expedition reports 1842 + 1843

- **What**: Frémont's full text, including the canonical descriptions of Devil's Gate, Independence Rock ("Great Register"), South Pass, Sweetwater, Soda Springs (NOT Soda Springs flagged here — Frémont 1843 Soda Springs WAS retrieved)
- **Where**: LOC + Project Gutenberg
- **Unlocks**: Multiple landmarks; resolves Frémont visit-year disambiguations
- **How to retrieve**: Project Gutenberg has Frémont — try pg.gutenberg.org/ebooks/search "Frémont expedition." Direct text download.

### Edwin Bryant — *What I Saw in California* (1848)

- **What**: Bryant 1846 traveled the full trail; his book describes every major landmark. Specific Independence Rock, Bear River, Big Blue, Courthouse Rock passages flagged as not-web-accessible.
- **Where**: Project Gutenberg + Internet Archive
- **Unlocks**: Multiple landmarks
- **How to retrieve**: pg.gutenberg.org/ebooks/search "Bryant California 1848." Free.

### Mary Stuart Bailey — 1852 diary (Independence Rock account)

- **What**: One of the best-described 1852 Independence Rock accounts
- **Where**: BEHIND PAYWALL — possibly JSTOR or a university library reprint
- **Unlocks**: Independence Rock
- **How to retrieve**: JSTOR access via public-library card (NYPL + many systems). Or interlibrary loan.

### Eliza Spalding diary — 1836 South Pass crossing

- **What**: First American woman through South Pass (with Narcissa Whitman)
- **Where**: Possibly Whitman College, Spalding family archives
- **Unlocks**: South Pass (resolves the second-hand "crossed a ridge of land today called the divide" attribution)
- **How to retrieve**: Contact Whitman College Penrose Library archives.

## 📚 Out-of-print scholarly monographs (interlibrary loan)

### Gowans & Campbell — *Fort Bridger* (1975)

- **What**: Definitive single-volume Fort Bridger history; resolves the Mormon purchase-vs-seizure debate + architectural details across all three period states
- **Where**: Wyoming State Library + Brigham Young University library + Marriott Library (Utah) + various academic libraries
- **Unlocks**: Fort Bridger (multi-state architectural specifics)
- **How to retrieve**: Interlibrary loan. ~3-week turnaround. Free.

### *At Waiilatpu* — Whitman College (1957)

- **What**: Anchor source on the active mission AND post-massacre ruin state
- **Where**: Whitman College Penrose Library, Walla Walla WA
- **Unlocks**: Whitman Mission (period diary quotes for the ruin state — the single biggest gap in that research file)
- **How to retrieve**: Interlibrary loan via the Whitman College library system.

### Crabtree 1988 — NPS Administrative History (Whitman Mission)

- **What**: Same gap-filler as *At Waiilatpu* for the ruin state
- **Where**: NPS administrative history series, online or interlibrary loan
- **Unlocks**: Whitman Mission ruin state
- **How to retrieve**: NPS administrative histories are often free PDFs at irma.nps.gov.

### Erwin N. Thompson NPS Study — Whitman Mission

- **What**: Detailed architectural reference (mussel-shell whitewash, HBC paint colors, T-shaped building dimensions, 16×30 ft blacksmith shop, 40-inch grinding stones, millpond dike system)
- **Where**: Already retrieved via Project Gutenberg — used in the Whitman research file. **No further retrieval needed.**

### Leo Dieker 1932 — Hollenberg Ranch monograph

- **What**: The most important specialized source on Hollenberg Ranch
- **Where**: Kansas Historical Society
- **Unlocks**: Hollenberg Ranch (verified dimensions, named riders, more 1850s-era diaries)
- **How to retrieve**: KSHS archives — direct request to kshs.org.

## 📑 Institutional PDFs (binary-encoded or paywalled)

### HABS KS-59 measured drawings — Hollenberg Ranch

- **What**: 10 measured architectural drawings of the intact original building, including exact dimensions, framing details, plan
- **Where**: Library of Congress, loc.gov/item/ks0146/
- **Unlocks**: Hollenberg Ranch (validates "28×40 ft" working dimensions in current research doc)
- **How to retrieve**: LOC HABS collection — drawings are usually downloadable as TIFFs; the URL is confirmed but sheets returned 403 to web fetch. Try LOC reader account or direct librarian email.

### Idaho State Historical Society — Reference Series PDFs on Fort Hall

- **What**: Specifically Reference Series No. 121 + the NHL nomination form
- **Where**: history.idaho.gov
- **Unlocks**: Fort Hall (ruin-state eyewitness diaries especially)
- **How to retrieve**: Direct librarian request — PDFs not web-accessible to anonymous tools.

### OCTA Shawnee County trail diaries PDF (Kansas River crossings)

- **What**: Compiled diary excerpts on the Kansas River crossings, including Pappan's Ferry
- **Where**: OCTA Shawnee County chapter, squarespace-hosted PDF
- **Unlocks**: Kansas River (additional diary attribution)
- **How to retrieve**: OCTA membership probably gets you the PDF; otherwise direct chapter contact.

### Bruff sketches (Library of Congress) — Ash Hollow, Devil's Gate

- **What**: At least two Ash Hollow drawings ("near Ash-Hollow") + Devil's Gate sketch
- **Where**: Library of Congress
- **Unlocks**: Ash Hollow, Devil's Gate
- **How to retrieve**: LOC reader account for higher-res access; direct librarian email for specific items.

## How Dave can act on this list

The single highest-leverage move would be:

1. **Order Bruff's *Gold Rush* via interlibrary loan** (or buy used) — that unlocks 7 of the 27 research files.
2. **Order Mattes's *The Great Platte River Road*** — unlocks the Platte cluster (Kearny → Robidoux).
3. **Request the Paul Kane Walla Walla sketches from Royal Ontario Museum** by curator email — single best Fort Walla Walla visual.
4. **Get JSTOR access via public-library card** (NYPL works) — unlocks Mary Stuart Bailey 1852 + several other paywalled academic-journal accounts.

After those four, the remaining gaps drop by ~80%. The rest are minor verifications.

This doc is the running checklist — strikethrough items as we retrieve them, add new gaps as new research files surface them.
